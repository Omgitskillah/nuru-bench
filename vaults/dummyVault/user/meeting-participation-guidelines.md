# Meeting Participation Guidelines

## Before Meetings
- Review agenda and related documents
- Prepare any questions or concerns
- Test technology/connectivity 30 minutes prior

## During Meetings
- Full attention - minimize distractions
- Take concise notes
- Speak up with ideas and concerns

## After Meetings
- Capture action items promptly
- Share notes within 24 hours
- Follow up on assigned tasks
