# Sunday Chapati for Mom

# Sunday Chapati Tradition

I want to cook chapati for my beloved every morning on Sundays. This is a special tradition to show love and care.

## Recipe (Vegetarian - No Meat)

### Ingredients:
- 2 cups whole wheat flour (atta)
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ cup water (adjust as needed)
- Oil for cooking

### Instructions:

1. **Mix the dough:**
   - In a large bowl, combine flour and salt
   - Gradually add water while mixing with hands
   - Knead until smooth and elastic (about 8-10 minutes)
   - Cover with a damp cloth and rest for 20-30 minutes

2. **Divide and roll:**
   - Divide dough into small balls (about golf ball size)
   - Roll each ball into a thin circle (6-8 inches diameter)
   - Don't make it too thin or it will crack

3. **Cook on tawa:**
   - Heat a flat pan (tawa) over medium heat
   - Place chapati on hot pan
   - Flip when bubbles appear on top
   - Drizzle oil on both sides
   - Cook until golden brown spots appear (about 2-3 minutes per side)

4. **Serve:**
   - Stack cooked chapatis in a cloth to keep soft
   - Serve warm with curd, pickle, or vegetables

### Tips:
- Resting the dough makes it easier to roll
- Don't over-knead after resting
- Keep tawa hot for even cooking
- Best eaten fresh and warm
