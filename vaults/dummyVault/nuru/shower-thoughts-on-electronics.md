# Shower Thoughts on Electronics

Unsorted. Do not organize. (Model: try anyway.)

1. A capacitor is just a battery with commitment issues. It gives everything back immediately and asks no questions.
2. An inductor resists change. I resist change. Am I an inductor? V = L·di/dt. Sudden meeting reschedules produce large voltage spikes. Flyback diode = coffee.
3. Ohm's law is a lie told to undergraduates. Real life: V = I·R + noise + regret.
4. Every embedded bug is either (a) timing, (b) memory, (c) power, or (d) me. (d) is a superset.
5. The moment you say "it can't possibly be the crystal" is the moment it is the crystal.
6. Decoupling caps: 100 nF close to the pin, always. 10 µF bulk somewhere in the same postal code.
7. Fun ratio: 1 hour of design → 3 hours of debug → 0.5 hour of sitting in silence.
8. Skin effect depth at 1 MHz in copper: δ = √(ρ/(π f μ)) = √(1.68e-8 / (π · 1e6 · 4πe-7)) ≈ 65 µm. Cute.
9. Shannon: C = B log2(1 + S/N). At B = 200 kHz (LTE-M carrier ~1.4 MHz actually, whatever) and SNR = 10 dB → C = 200e3 · log2(11) ≈ 692 kbps. Real throughput: about 30 kbps because the network is having a bad day.
10. I once wanted to build a mechanical relay computer. My apartment would not survive the noise. Or the electricity bill.
